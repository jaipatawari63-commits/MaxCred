# Daily MaxCred Workbook

The daily Excel master is the project control file. Sheet 1 is `Demo Tracker` and must be updated every working day.

Required sheets: Demo Tracker, Card Master, Master Progress, Data Completeness, Axis Current Snapshot, Axis Upcoming 28-Aug, Change Log, Daily Updates, Issuer Master, Issuer Sources, Official Card Findings, Research Control, Today Enumeration.

The workbook tracks both product launch readiness and card-data freshness. Current terms must not be overwritten by future effective terms. A card is recommendation-ready only when recommendation-critical economics are verified or explicitly N/A.
